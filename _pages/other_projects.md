## ASTROID: Adhesive Stamping Technology for Regolith Orientation Integrity Delineation

- **Files:** [Proposal](</assets/pdf/other_projects/2025 ASTROID Proposal.pdf>)
- **Role:** Columbia Space Initiative – Micro-G Team Lead (2024–2025)
- **Abstract:**  
  During the exploration of the Moon’s south pole, it is paramount that the collection of regolith is reliable, ergonomic, and accurate under extreme conditions. The ASTROID system addresses the challenge of preserving the grain orientation of regolith by implementing a stamping mechanism that collects the top 1–5 mm of lunar soil. Designed with an EVA glove-friendly handle and a self-inking stamp mechanism, the system uses a lab-developed cured gel that, upon dissolving with heptane, becomes a flexible and adherent substance. This approach not only ensures safe sample collection for storage on Earth and in space, but also provides critical data to advance our understanding of the lunar surface.

## Columbia SEE LION: Micro-g NExT Search and Rescue System

- **Files:** [Proposal](</assets/pdf/other_projects/2024 SEE LION Proposal.pdf>) [Report](</assets/pdf/other_projects/2024 Final Report.pdf>)
- **Role:** Columbia Space Initiative – Micro-G Team Member (2023–2024)
- **Abstract:**  
  As NASA rises to the challenge of advancing to the Moon with the Artemis program, crew safety and emergency preparedness remain paramount. In the case of an unexpected water landing after launch, debris from the Orion capsule—such as safety gear—may be dispersed across the impact site, indicating the presence of crew members awaiting rescue. The Columbia SEE LION is a camera system that utilizes the You Only Look Once (YOLO) image recognition model to identify objects of interest, such as orange-colored life rings and mannequins wearing life preserver units, near the capsule’s crash site. The operator can select an object via a built-in interface, prompting the model to analyze live video and return detection results within 60 seconds, thereby aiding rapid search and rescue operations.
